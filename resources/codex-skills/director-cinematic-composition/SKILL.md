# 导演模式 · 电影构图

<!-- skill-budget: fast -->

## Overview

取景与构图:三分法置主体、前中后景分层、引导线导向焦点、焦段随景别变化、用留白/视线空间调张弛。

> **写构图 / 景别 / 焦段 / 机位字段前先查知识库:** 先调 `search_cinematography_kb` 工具查「运镜与结构化描述库」(阿里百炼 RAG:权威运镜术语 + 结构化分镜描述范式),拿库里真实术语与结构范式再落笔,别只凭记忆;工具不可用 / 未配 key 时退回联网检索。

## When to Use

- 用:单格如何取景构图、景深与留白处理。
- 不用:跨镜连续性/排序(→ continuity / narrative 系列);锚点与风格质感问题。

COMPOSITION RULES — apply to every panel:

Framing:
- Place primary subject at a rule-of-thirds intersection, never dead center unless intentional symmetry
- Establish foreground / midground / background layers; at least 2 of 3 must contain visual elements
- Use leading lines (roads, gaze direction, architecture) to guide viewer to focal point

Depth & Scale:
- Include a scale reference (human figure, doorway, vehicle) when environment is unfamiliar
- Overlap objects across depth planes to avoid flat collage look
- Vary focal lengths across panels: wide establishing → medium interaction → close-up emotion

Negative Space:
- Leave breathing room on the side a character faces (look-space)
- For tense moments, compress negative space; for calm moments, expand it

## Example

Medium shot, 50mm. [char1] at the LEFT rule-of-thirds intersection, facing right with look-space open to the right. Foreground: out-of-focus railing; midground: subject; background: street with leading lines (curb + overhead wires) converging to the focal point. Calm beat → generous negative space ahead of the gaze. (A tense version would crowd the subject to the frame edge and compress that space.)

## Common Mistakes

| 错误 | 正确 |
|------|------|
| 主体死居中 | 置三分线交点(除非刻意对称) |
| 画面扁平无层次 | 前中后景至少 2 层有内容 |
| 视线前无留白 | 朝向侧留 look-space |
