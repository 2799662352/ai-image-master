# Instrument Palettes

Select palette by cultural context of the scene. Mix palettes for cross-cultural fusion.

## East Asian Classical

| Role | Instruments | Freq Range | Character |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Lead melody | Erhu, Dizi, Xiao, Pipa | 200Hz-2kHz | Lyrical, expressive vibrato |
| Harmonic bed | Guzheng, Guqin | 80Hz-1kHz | Arpeggiated, resonant decay |
| Rhythm | Bianzhong, Muyu, Qing | 500Hz-8kHz | Ritual, metallic, sustained |
| Bass | Dagu (large drum) | 40Hz-200Hz | Deep, reverberant impact |
| **Use for:** Ancient Chinese, wuxia, xianxia, court drama |

## Japanese Traditional

| Role | Instruments | Freq Range | Character |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Lead | Shakuhachi, Shamisen | 200Hz-4kHz | Breathy, plucked, raw |
| Rhythm | Taiko, Noh drums | 40Hz-1kHz | Thunderous or precise |
| Texture | Sho (mouth organ), Koto | 300Hz-3kHz | Ethereal, sustained chords |
| **Use for:** Jidaigeki, kaidan (ghost stories), samurai |

## Western Classical / Orchestral

| Role | Instruments | Freq Range | Character |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Lead | Violin, Cello, Oboe, French Horn | 60Hz-4kHz | Expressive, wide dynamic range |
| Harmonic | String quartet, Harp, Woodwinds | 80Hz-3kHz | Rich, layered, warm |
| Power | Brass (trumpet, trombone, tuba), Timpani | 40Hz-2kHz | Heroic, oppressive, ceremonial |
| Sacred | Pipe organ, Choir | 30Hz-8kHz | Transcendent, massive |
| **Use for:** European historical, period drama, epic, gothic |

## Modern Cinematic / Electronic

| Role | Instruments | Freq Range | Character |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Lead | Synth pads, Processed vocals | 40Hz-8kHz | Cold, vast, alien |
| Rhythm | Electronic drums, Glitch, Sub-bass pulse | 20Hz-4kHz | Mechanical, irregular, driving |
| Texture | Distorted guitar, Feedback, Tape loops | 100Hz-10kHz | Industrial, decayed |
| **Use for:** Sci-fi, cyberpunk, contemporary thriller, psychological |

## Minimalist / Ambient

| Role | Instruments | Freq Range | Character |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Lead | Solo piano, Solo cello | 60Hz-4kHz | Isolated, intimate, vulnerable |
| Texture | Room tone, Ambient electronics, Field recordings | 20Hz-2kHz | Space > sound, silence as instrument |
| **Use for:** Art house, indie, quiet drama, introspective |

## Ethnic Fusion

| Role | Instruments | Freq Range | Character |
|------|------------|------------|-----------|
| Lead | Any ethnic instrument + Western strings | varies | Cultural specificity + emotional universality |
| Rhythm | Ethnic percussion + electronic processing | 30Hz-8kHz | Ancient meets modern |
| **Use for:** Cross-cultural narratives, epic fantasy, world-building |
| **Key composers:** Tan Dun, Ryuichi Sakamoto, Gustavo Santaolalla |

## SFX / Foley Reference

|------------|-------------------|---------|
| Footstep | material + speed + rhythm | "bare foot on stone, 0.8Hz, echo decay 0.5s" |

| Metal | material + collision + overtone | "copper bell strike 3Hz, 4kHz overtone, decay 0.3s" |
